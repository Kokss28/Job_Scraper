# 🏢 Screen 3: Market & Geographic Hiring Hubs

---

## 🎯 Purpose & User Personas
Designed for **Talent Acquisition Directors, Compensation Analysts, and Recruiters** seeking macro-level market intelligence. It reveals the geographic concentration of tech opportunities across India, top hiring enterprises, and fast-growing technical competencies.

---

## ⚙️ Core Features & Analytics

### 1. Indian Tech Hubs Leaderboard
- Normalizes city and state abbreviations (e.g., `KA, IN` $\rightarrow$ `Karnataka / Bengaluru`, `TS, IN` $\rightarrow$ `Telangana / Hyderabad`, `TN, IN` $\rightarrow$ `Tamil Nadu / Chennai`).
- Interactive geographic ranking:
  - **Bengaluru (Silicon Plateau)**: Deep tech, backend, cloud, startups, product firms.
  - **Hyderabad (Cyberabad)**: Enterprise software, healthcare IT, global capability centers (GCCs).
  - **Pune & Mumbai**: Financial services tech, fintech, automotive embedded tech.
  - **Delhi NCR (Gurugram & Noida)**: Consumer internet, SaaS, ecommerce.
  - **Chennai**: Enterprise IT, automotive software, banking infrastructure.

### 2. Top Hiring Enterprises Leaderboard
- Identifies the most active hiring organizations in the catalog:
  - Global Capability Centers (Citi, JPMorgan Chase, Optum, Husky).
  - Indian IT Giants (Tata Consultancy Services, Infosys, Accenture in India).
  - Product & High-Growth Startups.
- Direct filtering: Click any company to isolate all of their active vacancies.

### 3. Comprehensive Tech Stack Landscape
- High-frequency skill ranking:
  - **Core Languages**: Python, Java, JavaScript/TypeScript, SQL, C++, Go.
  - **Frameworks & Libraries**: Django, FastAPI, React, Node.js, Spring Boot, Pandas/PyTorch.
  - **Cloud & DevOps**: AWS, Docker, Kubernetes, Azure, GCP, Jenkins.
  - **Data & AI**: Machine Learning, NLP, Spark, Airflow, Snowflake.

### 4. Qualification & Education Matrix
- Visual breakdown of minimum degrees demanded:
  - `B.Tech / B.E. (Bachelor of Technology / Engineering)`
  - `MCA / BCA (Computer Applications)`
  - `B.Sc / M.Sc (Science & Math)`
  - `Master's / Ph.D. / M.Tech`
